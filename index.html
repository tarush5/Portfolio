My Process

The same three steps take every project from raw data to a reliable product.

01

Frame & Explore

02

Model & Retrieve

Pick what fits: classical ML, deep learning, hybrid RAG with lexical and semantic retrieval, or multi-stage agentic workflows.

03

Ship & Harden

Serve through FastAPI, containerize with Docker, automate CI/CD and back it with tests so it stays reliable after launch.
